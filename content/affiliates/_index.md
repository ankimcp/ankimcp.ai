### 3. How Referrals Are Credited

- You get a unique referral link. When someone visits AnkiMCP through it, a referral cookie is stored in their browser for **60 days**.
- Your referral is saved to that person's AnkiMCP account the first time they start a checkout **on the web** while the cookie is present, and you are credited when they subscribe to a paid plan on the web through our checkout.
- **Not eligible:** subscriptions bought in the AnkiMCP app for Android through Google Play; purchases where no referral was saved because the referral cookie was blocked, deleted, or expired before their first web checkout; customers who were already paying subscribers before they used your link; and customers already linked to another affiliate.
- Generally, the first referral saved to the customer's account is credited, even if they later click another affiliate's link. If tracking data is missing or unclear, we decide which referral, if any, is credited, based on our records and Affonso's. Our decision is final.

### 4. Commission

- You earn **30%** of each payment a referred customer makes for their subscription, for the first **12 months** after their first payment. After 12 months, that customer no longer earns you commission.
- Commission is calculated on the amount actually paid after discounts, **excluding taxes** (such as VAT or sales tax) where our platform allows.
- A payment that is fully refunded, charged back, or reversed earns **no commission**; a partially refunded payment earns commission only on the part we keep. If it was already paid out to you, we deduct it from your future commissions.
- We may change the commission rate or duration for future referrals with at least **30 days'** notice by email. Commissions on payments made before the change are not affected.

### 5. Payouts

- A commission becomes payable **30 days** after the payment it is based on. This holding period covers our money-back guarantee and most refunds and chargebacks.
- Payable commissions are paid **monthly**, once your balance reaches **$50**. A smaller balance carries over to the next month.
- Payouts are made through Affonso, using the payout methods it offers. You are responsible for any fees your payout method charges.
- You are responsible for reporting and paying all taxes on your commissions. We may withhold payment until you provide any tax information we are required to collect.
